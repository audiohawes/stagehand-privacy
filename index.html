Stagehand

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Stagehand is a show-day tool for live-audio engineers and stage techs, made by Dan Hawes (AudioHawes). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Your show data stays on your own devices. Stagehand has no servers of ours, no accounts, and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your device is when you choose to — by syncing to your own nearby devices, sharing a pack you export, printing a label, or using the optional document reader (which uses your own Anthropic account).

What Stagehand stores

Everything you build — input lists and patches, stage plots, wireless plans, running orders, counts, and any show files you import — is stored locally on your device (and in your own iCloud or Files storage if you keep it there). We never receive a copy.

What we collect

Nothing. Stagehand has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no sign-in, and no user accounts. There is no server of ours that receives your data.

When data leaves your device — only when you choose

  • Local sync. Stagehand can sync a show between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network. This is device-to-device and does not pass through any server of ours.
  • Sharing. When you export an advance pack, or share a show as a PDF or a single self-contained web page, that file goes wherever you send it. You choose who receives it.
  • Printing. Labels print directly to a connected Brother P-touch printer over USB or Bluetooth.
  • Reading a document (optional). Stagehand can read a rider, input list, stage plot, schedule or hire quote and fill in your show. If you use this, the document you choose is sent to Anthropic’s Claude service (api.anthropic.com) using your own Anthropic API key, billed to your own Anthropic account, and handled under Anthropic’s privacy policy. This feature does nothing until you add your own key, and you pick which document to read each time. Only that document is sent — nothing else.

Documents you import

When you import a rider, plot or show file, Stagehand reads it to fill in your show and keeps the file on your device. The only time an imported document is sent anywhere is the optional reader described above, and only to Anthropic under your own account.

Children

Stagehand is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.
