A few common questions

  • Where do I start? On the Mac or iPad, tap Open the Example (sample data, nothing real) or make a New Tour or New Event. The iPhone app does not make new shows. It gets a show from Stagehand on your Mac over Wi-Fi, or opens a .stagehand file from Files.
  • Where does my show data live? On your own device. Stagehand has no account and no server of ours. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I move a show between my devices? Stagehand syncs between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi — no account needed.
  • Can it print labels? Yes — stage-box and in-ear labels print to a Brother P-touch over USB or Bluetooth.
  • What's the “Read a Document” reader? On the Mac, it fills in your show from a rider or plot using Apple’s on-device model, so nothing leaves your Mac. You can choose to use Claude instead with your own Anthropic API key; only then is the document you pick sent to Anthropic.
